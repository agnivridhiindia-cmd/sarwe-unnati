GOVERNANCE & RESILIENCE • SLIDE 19

Operational Risk Assessment & Mitigation Protocols.

Proactive contingency protocols engineered into our daily supply chain and financial management to guarantee unbroken relief.

HIGH PRIORITY • RISK 01 SUPPLY CHAIN

Supply Chain Disruption & Inflation

Surges in commodity grain, pulse, or fuel pricing threatening budget ceilings.

Mitigation Protocol Long-term forward contracts with regional APMC wholesale suppliers; 15-day dry grain buffer stock maintained in central Saharanpur facility.
HIGH PRIORITY • RISK 02 FOOD SAFETY

Food Safety & Temperature Spoilage

Risk of bacterial proliferation during summer transit and high humidity seasons.

Mitigation Protocol Strict adherence to FSSAI protocols, hot-holding at >65°C in insulated containers, 2-hour distribution window post-sourcing, and daily batch retention samples.
HIGH PRIORITY • RISK 03 CAPITAL FLOW

Funding Shortfall & Cash Flow Delays

CSR grant disbursement lag times or donor attrition during economic slowdowns.

Mitigation Protocol Multi-stream revenue portfolio across CSR grants, individual giving, and foundations. A 3-month operational reserve fund is systematically built.
MODERATE • RISK 04 STATUTORY

Regulatory & Statutory Compliance

Evolving MCA, Income Tax Form 10BD, and Section 8 governance requirements.

Mitigation Protocol Retained Chartered Accountants and legal counsel ensuring timely Form 10BD filings, MCA returns, 80G renewal certificates, and Section 8 compliance.
MODERATE • RISK 05 HUMAN CADRE

Volunteer Attrition & Field Fatigue

High volunteer turnover leading to dispatch inconsistencies or field delays.

Mitigation Protocol Rotating shifts, structured onboarding curriculum, formal recognition ceremonies, and certified community service accreditations.
MODERATE • RISK 06 OPERATIONS

Fleet Breakdown & Weather Extremes

Monsoon inundation or vehicular mechanical failure disrupting morning dispatches.

Mitigation Protocol Annual vehicle maintenance contracts (AMC), backup transport tie-ups with regional logistics operators, and rainproof insulated packaging.
