Sarwe Unnati Foundation (formerly Haridwar Education Foundation) was incorporated under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013 to bridge foundational educational, technological, and healthcare disparities across Northern India.

// OUR GENESIS & JOURNEY

From Local Classrooms to Regional Social Infrastructure.

Established in Saharanpur, Uttar Pradesh, on August 14, 2018 with Registration Number 106959 (RoC Kanpur), our organization began as Haridwar Education Foundation with a singular mandate: delivering remedial education to first-generation rural learners.

As our ground operations expanded to youth computer laboratories, mobile medical camps, and women self-help cooperatives, our name was formally transitioned to Sarwe Unnati Foundation—signifying universal progress and equitable empowerment for every stratum of society.

How We Operate Differently.
