🚀 GROUND INTERVENTIONS • 5 CORE PILLARS

Five Strategic Verticals.
One Unified Vision of Empowerment.

From grassroots primary classrooms to vocational computer laboratories and village preventive medical clinics, our programs are engineered to build self-sustaining community resilience.

VERTICAL 01 • FOUNDATIONAL EDUCATION

Shiksha Unnati: Universal Schooling & Learning Kits.

Quality early education is the bedrock of social mobility. Shiksha Unnati delivers foundational literacy, distributes complete study kits (stationery, backpacks, textbooks), and runs community remedial study centers in remote hamlets where first-generation learners require focused academic support.

✓ 5,000+ students supported with complete study kits
✓ Evening remedial study centers with trained local educators
✓ Parent-teacher counseling for dropout prevention
VERTICAL 02 • TECHNOLOGY & IT SKILLING

Kaushal Unnati: Digital Labs & Youth Employment.

In an increasingly digital economy, lack of computer access limits rural youth to low-wage manual labor. Kaushal Unnati establishes modern computer labs equipped with hardware, internet, and structured curricula spanning basic computing, office software, networking, and digital literacy.

✓ Practical IT labs established across semi-urban clusters
✓ 1,200+ rural youth certified in vocational computing
✓ Resume assistance, mock interviews, and regional job placement
VERTICAL 03 • HEALTHCARE & MEDICINE

Aarogya Unnati: Rural Clinics & Preventive Health.

Preventable diseases cause severe financial strain on underprivileged families. Aarogya Unnati organizes mobile diagnostic camps led by verified physicians—providing free blood tests, eye screenings, pediatric checkups, and prescription medicine distributions directly in remote villages.

✓ 35+ full-scale rural medical camps conducted
✓ Free consultation, basic diagnostics, and prescription medicines
✓ Clean drinking water drives and seasonal hygiene awareness
VERTICAL 04 • WOMEN EMPOWERMENT

Mahila Sashaktikaran: Self-Help Groups & Micro-Enterprise.

When women earn, entire communities transform. We establish tailoring cooperatives, provide sewing machines, teach financial literacy and basic accounting, and organize women into Self-Help Groups (SHGs) to achieve financial independence.

✓ Tailoring & embroidery centers with modern sewing units
✓ Financial literacy, bank account opening, and micro-savings
✓ Direct market linkage for artisanal handmade products
VERTICAL 05 • RURAL WELFARE & ENVIRONMENT

Gramin Utthan: Village Infrastructure & Plantation.

Grassroots development requires resilient infrastructure. Gramin Utthan undertakes environmental tree plantation drives, community sanitation improvements, solar lighting installations, and emergency relief distribution during severe weather and floods.

✓ 15,000+ native saplings planted across rural greening corridors
✓ Village sanitation units and clean community water stations
✓ Disaster relief kits with dry rations, blankets, and hygiene supplies
