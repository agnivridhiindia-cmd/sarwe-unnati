FSSAI COMPLIANCE • HYGIENE FRAMEWORK

Rigorous Food Safety & Quality Standards.

Food safety and clinical hygiene are non-negotiable across every phase of our food sourcing intake, thermal transit, and field dispatch network.

✓

Verified Sourcing & Culinary Partners

All prepared meals are sourced exclusively from licensed, vetted commercial food partners, banquet surplus recovery tie-ups, and institutional culinary facilities adhering to stringent FSSAI hygiene standards.

✓

FSSAI Compliance & Food Quality Audits

All food batches undergo strict intake screening under Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) guidelines, including thermal logging and certified partner lab audits.

✓

Insulated Thermal Transit & Stainless Vessels

Heavy-gauge food-grade 304 stainless steel containers and vacuum-insulated cambros keep food safe and steaming hot at >65°C throughout all transit routes.

✓

100% Biodegradable Compostable Packaging

Zero single-use plastics. Meals are dispatched in biodegradable bagasse fiber plates and unbleached paper sleeves, preventing ecological contamination.

CLINICAL SAFETY FIRST

Daily Batch Quality Sampling

Every single food batch undergoes strict temperature verification (>65°C) and organoleptic taste testing at intake before leaving the dispatch bay.

LOGISTICAL ROADMAP • SLIDE 10

Three-Phase Distribution Blueprint.

The foundation executes a phased logistical roadmap to maximize field outreach while systematically minimizing distribution operating overhead.

01
PHASE 01 • PILOT

Pilot Field Network

Stress-test batch portion calibration, temperature stability, and transit routes across 2 to 3 high-need urban clusters in Saharanpur.

  • 150–300 Meals Sourced & Distributed Daily
  • 2–3 Dedicated Daily Dispatch Hubs
  • 100% Baseline Recipe Calibration
02
PHASE 02 • SCALE & EXPANSION

Full Operational Scale

Transition to the permanent ₹2 Crore target run-rate of 822 nutritious hot meals daily, utilizing customized insulated delivery vans and standardized route registries.

  • 822 Meals / Day (3,00,000 / Year)
  • 5–10 Fixed Daily Distribution Points
  • Dedicated Insulated Mobile Van Fleet
03
PHASE 03 • SUSTAINABILITY

Regional Ecosystem

Establish permanent multi-year institutional alliances with municipal welfare bodies, regional mandis, commercial food rescue donors, and corporate CSR committees.

  • 100+ Trained Volunteer Cadre
  • 30+ MT Food Surplus Diverted / Year
  • Section 135 Corporate Multi-Year CSR
