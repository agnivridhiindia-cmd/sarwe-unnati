+ GET TO KNOW US

Sarwe Unnati Foundation represents a disciplined paradigm in grassroots social development. Established under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013, we engineer sustainable, accountable interventions across foundational education, youth IT skilling, and rural healthcare throughout Uttar Pradesh and Northern India.

01 // EDUCATION

Shiksha Unnati

Universal schooling, backpacks, stationery kits, and remedial centers for 5,000+ students.

02 // TECHNOLOGY

Kaushal Unnati

Dedicated computer IT labs, software training, and job-readiness bootcamps for youth.

03 // HEALTHCARE

Aarogya Unnati

Free mobile health camps, specialist doctor checkups, diagnostic tests, and medicine distribution.

04 // STATUTORY 80G

50% Tax Relief
