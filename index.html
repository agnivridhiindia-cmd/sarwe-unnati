ACUTE GROUND REALITY • URGENT HUNGER CRISIS

The Harsh Reality of Urban Starvation.

Hunger remains an unrelenting barrier for people living on streets and in informal settlements. For hundreds of daily wagers and destitute citizens, securing even one wholesome meal each day is a battle.

01

Severe Nutritional & Protein Deficit

Street diets are heavily imbalanced, relying on low-cost carbohydrates with almost zero protein, dietary fiber, or vital micronutrients.

02

Irregular Meal Cadence

Homeless people and casual laborers frequently endure 24 to 48 hours without food, surviving on sporadic, unorganized charity drives.

03

Under-Served Micro-Pockets

Traditional feeding centers miss high-need labor squares, railway track dwellers, and temporary migrant construction clusters.

04

Severe Food Wastage Paradox

Tons of edible surplus food is discarded daily from commercial banquets while citizens sleep hungry just kilometers away.

05

Vulnerable Children & Destitute Seniors

Street children suffer irreversible cognitive stunting, while elderly citizens face severe malnutrition without family support.

THE FOUNDATION BRIDGE

Organized, Accountable Food Relief

SARWE UNNATI FOUNDATION bridges this gap with scheduled daily dispatches, approved culinary partners, and FSSAI hygiene standards.

12-MONTH TIMELINE • SLIDE 17

Implementation Roadmap.

A phased, disciplined rollout ensuring supplier verification, pilot validation, and measured expansion across Western UP.

01
MONTH 1

Foundational Setup & Ground Baseline

  • Location mapping and ward-level hunger baseline surveys across Saharanpur
  • FSSAI certified commercial kitchen agreements and equipment procurement
  • Wholesale ingredient supplier contracts for bulk pulses, grains, and spices
  • Ground volunteer mobilization drive and safety training onboarding
02
MONTHS 2–3

Pilot Operations & Portion Calibration

  • Launch pilot meal preparation batches (200–400 meals/day)
  • Calibrate standardized portioning (~650 kcal) and nutritional balances
  • Test thermal insulation containers and mobile transit dispatch routes
  • Establish daily physical recipient registration protocols at pilot hubs
03
MONTHS 4–6

Full-Scale 822 Meals/Day Rollout

  • Ramp operations to full annual target volume (~822 nutritious meals every day)
  • Activate 5–10 fixed distribution hubs covering labor chowks and night shelters
  • Initiate banquet and institutional cafeteria surplus food recovery network
  • Publish first quarterly independent CA audit report and donor briefing
04
MONTHS 7–9

Corporate CSR Alliances & Logistics Optimization

  • Formalize institutional CSR grant partnerships under Schedule VII Item (i)
  • Introduce automated route telemetry for dispatch vans to minimize idle transit
  • Expand volunteer base to 100 active community members with specialized PPE
  • Conduct mid-term nutritional health assessment among regular beneficiary groups
05
MONTHS 10–12

Impact Consolidation & Year 2 Expansion

  • Complete annual distribution milestone target of 3,00,000 wholesome meals
  • Publish comprehensive Annual Impact Dossier and statutory Form 10BE filing
  • Reconcile ₹2 Crore budget utilization certificate with statutory auditors
  • Finalize expansion blueprint for Phase II regional kitchen clusters
